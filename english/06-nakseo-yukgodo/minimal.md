## 圓束樣式

> Source: transcription of the original-text format from page 48 of the published translation of the 九數略, Kun (坤) volume (tr. Jeong Hae-nam and Heo Min, Gyowusa). This document is independent of BING_PARALLEL_OCR.md (direct OCR of the original Bing volume).

 ![minimal](./minimal.md)

今有方箭束一百四十四隻

Suppose there is a bundle of square arrows (方箭) numbering 144.

問外周幾何

How many make up the outer perimeter?

一 一

1, 1

二 二千二百八十八 列積減一以十六乗之

2, 2288. Lay out the product obtained above, subtract 1, then multiply by 16.

三 八 從方

3, 8 — following the side (從方).

四 四十四

4, 44.
