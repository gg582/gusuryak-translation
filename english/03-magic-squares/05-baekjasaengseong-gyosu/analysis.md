# Baekjasaengseong-gyosu (Hundred-Numbers Crossed-Numbers Diagram, 百子生成交數圖)

Order: 10×10

## Transcription and classification

Baekjasaengseong-gyosu records each cell as an **ordered pair**, `(a,b)`: it is a pair-grid. Collapsing a pair into a decimal integer such as `10a+b` loses the source's notation, so it must not be analysed as a normal 1–100 magic square or tested against a row sum of 505.

The supplied pair transcription is complete as it stands. The earlier substitutions (`16→11`, `85→86`, and changes intended to enforce a symmetry) are not source corrections and are not applied. There is consequently no corrected numerical edition.

## Properties

- Magic square: no. Sums of rows, columns, and diagonals are not defining conditions of this pair-grid.
- Normal 1–100 set: not applicable. A cell is a combination of two components, not a single card value.
- 50-orthogonal Latin squares: yes. This is a superposition of two order-ten Latin squares in which 50 ordered pairs each occur twice, rather than the 100 pairs each occurring once required for complete orthogonality.
- Proper subject of analysis: the two component arrays and their 50-orthogonal repetitions and placement—not a fabricated single-number array or a reconstructed numerical symmetry.

The diagram is therefore best described as a non-magic, order-ten 50-orthogonal Latin-square pair, rather than as a defective order-ten magic square.
