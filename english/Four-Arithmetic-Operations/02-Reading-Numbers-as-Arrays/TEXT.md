
## The Nine-Nine Multiplication Table

![九九母數名圖](../../../korean/총론사법/02-수를-배열로-읽는-법에-대하여/gugu_mosu_myeongdo.png)

上為綱數

The upper number is the gang (綱, guiding) number,

下為目數

and the lower number is the mok (目, subordinate) number.

*In biology and scholarly classification, the gang is the higher-ranking concept and the mok is the lower-ranking one.*

![九九母數象圖](../../../korean/총론사법/02-수를-배열로-읽는-법에-대하여/gugu_mosu_sangdo.png)

右為目數

The right-hand number is the mok number,

左為綱數

and the left-hand number is the gang number.

夫綱而妻目也

The gang number is the husband number, and the mok number is the wife number.

圖即蔡氏範數方圖

The diagram is the "square diagram of model numbers" (範數方圖) spoken of by Master Cai (Cai Chen, a Southern Song philosopher and mathematician).

![九九子數名圖](../../../korean/총론사법/02-수를-배열로-읽는-법에-대하여/gugu_jasu_myeongdo.png)

自上而下橫格有九自右而左直行有九綱第一直行為目直行自上看其相值處即相乘數
Nine horizontal rows are laid from top to bottom, and nine vertical columns are laid from right to left. The first vertical column is taken as the header of the rows and read from the top down; in each cell where a row and a column meet, write the product of the two numbers.

*It is the product of the two members of a pair from the transposed matrix.*

![九九子數象圖](../../../korean/총론사법/02-수를-배열로-읽는-법에-대하여/gugu_jasu_sangdo.png)

右為單數
The right-hand digit is the units place.
左為十數
The left-hand digit is the tens place.
從一而衡十也
Follow the one and lay it across horizontally, and it becomes the tens place.

*You have read more than a hundred lines of Classical Chinese, but all you have actually done is memorize the multiplication table.*

致加减者存乎

Is there one who brings addition and subtraction to their utmost?

乗除妙乗除者存乎

Is there one who makes the marvel of multiplication and division reside in multiplication and division?

九九如言九九八十一九

The "nine-nine" (九九) is like saying "nine nines are eighty-one (9×9=81)":

九者九箇九也九九母數也八十一子數也

"nine-nine" means that there are nine nines. The "nine-nine" (9×9) is the mother number (母數, the original number), and the "eighty-one" (81) is the child number (子數, the number born as the result).

如言八九七十二八九者八箇九也九箇八亦為七十二也以八乗九生七十二

Likewise, in saying "eight nines are seventy-two (8×9=72)", "eight-nine" means that there are eight nines, and nine eights also make seventy-two. Multiplying nine by eight gives birth to seventy-two.

八為夫而九為妻也

(Here) the 8 in front becomes the husband (the multiplier, the number that multiplies), and the 9 behind becomes the wife (the multiplicand, the number being multiplied).

諸數放此有四十五句或該而或省者圖以揭其全訣以提其要也

All the numbers follow this example, making forty-five verses in all; that some are set out in full while others are trimmed and omitted
is so that the diagrams may reveal the whole picture, and the chant may lift out its essentials.
