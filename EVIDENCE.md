# Evidence Status and Scope of Claims

This repository distinguishes source evidence from later mathematical and computational work. The labels below are used in new and revised documentation; older documents without line-by-line labels should be read with the same distinction.

| Label | Meaning | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Direct source** | A readable character, number, diagram feature, or cited primary/secondary text. | An inferred procedure or authorial intention. |
| **Reading / transcription** | A best-supported reading of damaged, ambiguous, or OCR-assisted material. | Certainty beyond the visible evidence. |
| **Structural hypothesis** | An explicitly introduced model used to investigate a diagram. | That the historical author used that model. |
| **Mathematical consequence** | A theorem or calculation following from stated premises. | That its premises are historical facts. |
| **Computational experiment** | The result of code, a solver, or an enumerated finite model. | A claim outside the encoded model, including uniqueness unless proved. |
| **Rejected reconstruction** | A previously considered digital reading or model that lacked source support or failed a stated test. | Evidence for the remaining hypotheses. |

Terms such as *reconstruction*, *witness solution*, and *optimal* are local to their stated assumptions and objective functions. They do not by themselves identify an original layout, generation rule, or authorial intention.

See [METHOD.md](METHOD.md) for the transcription workflow and the relevant chapter documentation for individual assumptions and tests.
